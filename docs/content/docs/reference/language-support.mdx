---
title: Language Support
description: "What each language does in a Jazz app, and what it doesn't support yet, compared with TypeScript."
---

Every language works from your app's TypeScript schema and permissions: `npx jazz-tools generate`
writes each language's typed bindings from them. Your app's main backend runs in TypeScript
([Vercel](/docs/setup/deployment/vercel) or [your own server](/docs/setup/deployment/own-server)).

Rust, Swift, Kotlin and Go are **experimental**: they are clients of that TypeScript backend. All
four can be apps, signed in as one of your users; Rust and Go also run as
[backend clients](/docs/setup/backend-client), services that act with a machine credential. None of
them runs a main backend yet. Each tab lists what that language supports and what it doesn't yet,
compared with TypeScript.

<Tabs groupId="jazz-environment" items={["Rust", "Swift", "Kotlin", "Go"]} persist updateAnchor>
  <Tab value="Rust">
    **Experimental.** Rust is a client of your TypeScript main backend's `/jazz`, in two roles: an
    app signed in as one of your users (`Config::user`, with device accounts or bearer sign-in, and
    `JazzSession`), or a backend client, a service with a machine credential, trusted or scoped, with
    `for_user` on a trusted client. It has queries with includes, `select`, hops, gathers and unions,
    history reads and subscriptions, partial reads (`Query::part`), branches and drafts, streaming
    uploads, mergeable text, permission advice, guests (device accounts), positions in subscription
    deltas, and `close(timeout)`. See [Rust](/docs/setup/rust) and
    [Backend Client](/docs/setup/backend-client).

    **Known issue in Rust apps built for Android:** `Storage::Dir` fails to open with
    `try_lock_shared() not supported`, in every release up to and including 2.0.0-beta.0.

    **Known issue in Rust apps built for Android:** `DeviceKey::load_or_create` can't create a key
    (reproduced on API 23, 24, 29, 30 and 35 emulators; other versions untested) and fails with
    `Permission denied (os error 13)`, in every release since 2.0.0-alpha.66. Use
    `DeviceKey::generate()` and keep the key's text form in the platform's secure store instead
    (untested on Android).

    It doesn't support yet:

    - running your app's main backend (serving its `/jazz` and publishing its permissions): run it
      in TypeScript with `createJazz`;
    - a built-in secure store (keychain) for device keys: the app keeps the key itself;
    - cookie sessions renewed in place: a Rust client signs in with a bearer and reconnects with a
      fresh one;
    - the browser-only parts of TypeScript's session: focus and visibility re-checks, cross-tab
      sign-out, `clearOnSignOut` and the thin-client upgrade.

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Swift">
    **Experimental.** Swift is for macOS and iOS apps: a client of your app, signed in as one of its
    users through your TypeScript main backend's `/jazz`. It has queries with includes, hops, unions
    and history reads, writes and uploads, mergeable and serializable transactions, subscriptions
    (`subscribe` and `watch`), mergeable text (`JazzText`, with `String.Index` or `NSRange`
    positions), permission advice (`canInsert`…), device accounts and bearer sign-in, sessions that
    follow who is signed in (`JazzApp.session`), sync status, `disconnect`/`reconnect`,
    `deleteClientStorage` and refused-write reports (`mutationErrors`). In SwiftUI, `JazzRoot` is
    TypeScript's `<JazzProvider>`, `@JazzAll` is `useAll`, and `@Environment(\.jazz)` and
    `@Environment(\.jazzSession)` are `useDb` and `useJazzSession`. It installs as one package,
    `garden-co/jazz-swift`, with the core prebuilt for macOS and iOS; each release's package is
    tested on macOS and an iPhone simulator, with a SwiftUI app UI-tested there. See
    [Swift](/docs/setup/swift).

    It doesn't support yet:

    - running a backend in Swift (publishing permissions, acting for users) or serving the app's
      Jazz endpoint: run your backend in TypeScript;
    - watchOS, tvOS and visionOS;
    - Linux: there's no prebuilt core. Ask on [Discord](https://discord.gg/RN9UKh52be) if you need
      it;
    - a SwiftUI `Binding` for `JazzText`;
    - Keychain (or file) storage for device keys;
    - anonymous (guest) users;
    - subscribing to history queries;
    - parts of large values inside includes, `offset` in an include, and `required` on reverse
      relations or optional references;
    - payload enums, nested rows and other column types the bindings can't type;
    - non-finite floats (`NaN`, infinities), which read back as `null`;
    - positions in subscription deltas.

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Kotlin">
    **Experimental.** Kotlin is for apps, on Android and the JVM: a client of your app, signed in as
    one of its users through your TypeScript main backend's `/jazz`. See [Kotlin](/docs/setup/kotlin).

    **Known issue on Android:** `Storage.Persistent` fails to open with
    `try_lock_shared() not supported`, in every release up to and including 2.0.0-beta.0. Until this
    is fixed, use the default `Storage.Memory` (tested on an API 35 x86_64 emulator). It loses its
    data, including writes not yet synced, when you close `Jazz` or the app's process ends. The JVM
    on desktop isn't affected.

    It doesn't support yet:

    - running a backend in Kotlin: run your backend in TypeScript;
    - reading part of a large value inside an include (root queries only);
    - guest (anonymous) users;
    - nested rows, catalogue enums and transaction-id columns in bindings;
    - Kotlin Multiplatform targets other than the JVM and Android;
    - non-finite doubles (`NaN`, infinities);
    - `limit` and `offset` with `watch`: use them with `all`.

  </Tab>
  <Tab value="Go">
    **Experimental.** Go is for apps, and runs as a backend client: a Go program can be a client of
    your app, signed in as one of its users, or a service that reads and writes through your
    TypeScript main backend's `/jazz` with a machine credential. See [Go](/docs/setup/go) and
    [Backend Client](/docs/setup/backend-client).

    It doesn't support yet:

    - running your main backend in Go (publishing permissions, serving `/jazz`): run it in
      TypeScript with `createJazz`;
    - guest (anonymous) users;
    - nested rows, catalogue enums and transaction-id columns: Go bindings are refused for apps that
      declare them;
    - non-finite doubles (`NaN`, `±Inf`): Jazz carries them as `null`, so a nullable `float` column
      reads them as `nil`, and reading a non-nullable one fails with a `*jazz.Error` that names the
      column.

  </Tab>
</Tabs>
