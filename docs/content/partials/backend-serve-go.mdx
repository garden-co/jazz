A Go backend serves the Jazz endpoint its end users' clients (TypeScript, Swift, Kotlin, Go) sign in
and sync through, as `createJazz` does in TypeScript. `jazz.NewEndpoint` is an `http.Handler`;
mount it at `/jazz`, and say who a request is for in your `authenticate`:

In a route of your own, `endpoint.ForUser(r)` is a handle acting as the request's user (TypeScript's
`forReqUser`); close it when done.
