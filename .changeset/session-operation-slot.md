The session now runs one operation at a time, with explicit rules for what may start while another runs. `signOut()` takes over from a sign-in, link or `refresh()` still running: they reject with the new `JazzSessionOperationError` (`reason: "superseded"`), and nothing they finish later signs anyone back in (a refresh whose answer arrived after a sign-out used to sign the user in again). Sign-ins and `refresh()` during a sign-out reject with `reason: "signing-out"`; a sign-in during another one rejects with `reason: "pending"` (same message as before). A `refresh()` asked during a sign-in or link waits for it, and refreshes asked while one runs share one more.

**Breaking:** `JazzSessionSnapshot.status` has a new value, `"signing-out"` (code that switches over every status must handle it), and an offline `signOut()` now resolves instead of rejecting. A sign-out that can't upload the user's last writes (offline) no longer fails and leaves the user signed in: it signs out on this device, `signOut()` resolves, and the session reports the new status `"signing-out"` until those writes upload (tried again with backoff, on reconnect, or by `retry()`), then `"signed-out"`. Providers show their `signedOut` view meanwhile. `signOut({ discardUnsynced: true })` signs out without waiting, also from `"signing-out"`: it loses the signed-out user's writes the server hasn't confirmed and wipes their local data from this device, so another user can sign in while offline. If another browser tab still has that account open, its data stays for that tab, and the session says so (`error` on `signed-out`); `retry()` finishes the wipe once that tab has closed. The pending wipe is kept on the device, so a reload or another page finishes it once no tab has the account open, and signing in as that account again cancels it. A wipe that fails stays pending and visible (`error` on `signed-out`) until `retry()` succeeds. Signing in as a device account just as another tab's discard wipes it fails with the new `DiscardedDeviceAccountError` instead of opening an empty store. The signed-out user's credential stays valid only until their last upload ends.
