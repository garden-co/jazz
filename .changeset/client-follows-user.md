Browser and React Native clients follow the user your backend names: your backend's `authenticate` is the only source of who is signed in. Jazz asks your backend on start, when the page or app returns to the foreground and when another tab signs in or out; there a signed-out answer signs out only once a second answer agrees. After your own sign-in, sign-up, sign-out or account switch, call `reauthenticate()` (from `useJazzAuth()`, on a session, or on `createJazzApp`'s app) once your session cookie or bearer is fresh: Jazz asks your backend and follows its answer at once. `useJazzAuth()` exposes that answer as `user` (`{ id, issuer, claims }`, or `null` for a guest or no one). Apps where only your backend's users have accounts pass `initial="signed-out"`; the default stays a local-first guest. Signing out, or switching to another user, closes that account's client; in browsers, `clearOnSignOut` wipes its local storage for shared devices, after waiting up to 5 seconds for pending writes to upload (not yet supported in React Native). `createJazz` re-runs `authenticate` every `recheckSeconds` (default 60) and ends a sync connection whose user it no longer names. The client reconnects with fresh cookies; if no one is signed in, that reconnect fails as an auth failure and the client stops retrying, reconnecting with backoff while the backend still names the same user. A refusal alone never signs anyone out: that waits for a check after focus, or 30 seconds later on a visible page.
