Browser and React Native clients follow the user your auth library has signed in. Every framework's `JazzProvider` takes `user` (`undefined` while your auth loads, `null` when signed out, the user's id when signed in), and sessions have `setUser(…)`. Without either, Jazz asks your backend who is signed in on start, when the page or app returns to the foreground and when another tab signs in or out; a signed-out answer signs out only once a second answer agrees. Auth libraries that refresh their session cookie in the browser (Clerk, for example) should pass `user` or call `setUser`. Signing out, or switching to another user, closes that account's client; in browsers, `clearOnSignOut` wipes its local storage for shared devices, after waiting up to 5 seconds for pending writes to upload (not yet supported in React Native). `createJazz` re-runs `authenticate` every `recheckSeconds` (default 60) and ends a sync connection whose user it no longer names. The client reconnects with fresh cookies; if no one is signed in, that reconnect fails as an auth failure and the client stops retrying, reconnecting with backoff while the backend still names the same user. A refusal alone never signs anyone out: that waits for a check after focus, or 30 seconds later on a visible page.
